# vessel_services_inferred

Status: **NOT READY** · verification: **NEEDS_ORGANISER** · private: no

_Generated by `scripts/update_data_status.py`. Unless the folder holds the files itself, the data stays at the paths below (scripts and tests read them there); this page documents them._

## What it is

- `vessel_corridor_services`: 32 inferred services for Mathiveri, Bodufolhudhoo, Ukulhas, Rasdhoo, Malé, Thoddoo, Maafushi; every service inferred: true.
- `vessel_service_signatures`: 19 ordered-stop signatures with class mix, days seen and device counts.
- `vessel_service_departures`: Per service and stop: median and p10-p90 time, days seen, weekday counts.
- `vessel_service_edges`: Island-pair and class level services per observed day, first/last departures, duration.
- `vessel_service_days`: Signature x date observed/not_observed with coverage flag.
- `vessel_inferred_vs_mtcc`: Published MTCC departures beside inferred services on the same island pairs, with wait to the next published departure.
- `vessel_jetty_selection`: Every harbour reference row considered for the vessel stops, with decision and reason.
- `vessel_service_summary`: Parameters, counts, disclosure rule, suppression counts, time_basis, clock correction, flags timezone_confirmed and speed_unit_confirmed.
- `vessel_hour_profiles`: Information only: trip departures by hour (raw vs corrected) and services whose first-stop median is 22:00-05:00.
- `vessel_clock_offsets`: National distribution of database_time - collection_time, by network and day; clock class counts. 0 device-days corrected.
- `followme_vessel_tables`: Per-device classes, 1,305 trips and 1,137 journeys with device keys.

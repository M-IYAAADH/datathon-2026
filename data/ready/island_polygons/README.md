## What it is

- `onemap_island_footprints`: OneMap island polygons for AA, ADh, K, V and Greater Malé (379 features); FCODE LD#### equals the Census island code.
- `ocha_cod_ab_boundaries`: Government of Maldives / OCHA COD-AB v02 administrative boundaries, as downloaded.
- `study_area_polygon`: Union of reviewed COD-AB polygons supplemented by all 379 OneMap footprints; defines the FollowMe regional selection.
- `study_island_coverage`: Per OneMap island: which study atoll it belongs to and whether COD-AB covers it.
- `geography_metadata`: Provenance, hashes and raw service metadata for the polygon layers.
